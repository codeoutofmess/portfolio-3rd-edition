<Step n="01" title="The problem">
No style guide and no component library. Fast growth had left three fonts, a palette that kept drifting, and buttons that did the same job but looked different.
</Step>

<Step n="02" title="Audit first">
I used the site like a customer, then went back screen by screen, noting what repeats and what's a one off. Guessing at a system without that list just makes another inconsistent one.
</Step>

<Media src="/images/work/beatstars-design-system/cover.png" caption="Components taken from the live site." />

<Step n="03" title="The system">
Five foundations (colour, type, icons, photography and voice) and five components (buttons, tags, cards, badges and controls). Every one uses variants that had already shipped, so engineering wouldn't have to redo work.
</Step>

<Step n="04" title="What I learned">
A system that doesn't match what ships is worth very little. The urge to tidy things up while documenting is strong, and mostly wrong.
</Step>
