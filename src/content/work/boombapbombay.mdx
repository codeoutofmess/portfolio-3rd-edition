<Step n="01" title="The brief">
KSHAH came to me for a website. It had to feel like the music: dusty boom bap drums, golden era hip hop, Mumbai noise and pulp crime imagery.
</Step>

<Step n="02" title="Designed and built by me">
I did the identity, the UI and the motion, then built it myself. With no handoff, every hover and scroll effect had to hold up on an old Android phone on patchy 4G, not just look good in Figma.
</Step>

<Media src="/images/work/boombapbombay/drop.jpg" caption="Each drop gets its own spread, with a preview and buy buttons." />

<Media src="/images/work/boombapbombay/lineup.jpg" caption="The beat line up." />

<Step n="03" title="A revolver that follows you">
The landing page has a 3D revolver that aims wherever your cursor goes, compressed so it loads fast. Scrolling runs on GSAP with Lenis, and phones get their own simpler scroll where pinned sections would jitter.
</Step>

  <Media src="/images/work/boombapbombay/made-to-order.jpg" caption="Custom beats, made to order." />
  <Media src="/images/work/boombapbombay/got-bars.jpg" caption="Got bars? We got beats." />
</Pair>

<Step n="04" title="A store you can trust">
Cart, Razorpay checkout and paid downloads. The server checks every payment signature, works out the price itself instead of trusting the browser, and hands out download links that expire. The payment checks have unit tests.
</Step>
