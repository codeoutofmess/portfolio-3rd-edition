<Step n="01" title="The question">
How do you get the information that matters about local candidates to the people voting for them?
</Step>

  <Media src="/images/work/candidacy-information/existing-resources.png" caption="One of the few guides voters have today." />
  <Media src="/images/work/candidacy-information/solution-concept.png" caption="How it works now: 150 words and a photo, by email." />

<Step n="02" title="Research">
Desk research, interviews on the street in Wellington and an hour with a sitting candidate. She explained the pamphlet is a word limit, a photo and an email deadline, which is why it says so little. Voters told us they wanted to compare candidates side by side.
</Step>

<Pair>
  <Media src="/images/work/candidacy-information/sketches.webp" caption="First sketches of the form." />
  <Media src="/images/work/candidacy-information/wireframe.png" caption="A rough wireframe, made to be broken in testing." />
</Pair>

<Step n="03" title="What testing caught">
Four things: people couldn't tell what the word limit applied to, "background" meant something different to everyone, required fields weren't marked, and the upload area didn't look clickable.
</Step>

<Media src="/images/work/candidacy-information/usability-findings.webp" caption="The four problems testing found." />

<Step n="04" title="The prototype">
