<Step n="01" title="The problem">
Only a few articles could be reached from the menu or topic pages. The rest depended on Google finding them, because the old CMS kept everything flat. The move to a new CMS was the one chance to fix that.
</Step>

<Media src="/images/work/consumer-nz-ia/research-methods.png" caption="Five research methods, each with its own job." />

<Step n="02" title="Research">
We compared other big content sites, audited the content, interviewed staff, ran card sorts and tested the existing site. The card sorts showed that the way staff organise articles and the way readers look for them were two different things.
</Step>

<Media src="/images/work/consumer-nz-ia/research-link-diagram.svg" caption="Where staff needs and reader needs pointed at the same problem." />

<Step n="03" title="What we changed">
Navigation built around the topics people search for, search with filters and grouped results, a homepage that gives reviews and articles equal weight, breadcrumbs, and tags that connect related research.
</Step>

<Step n="04" title="What I learned">
It was my first time leading a team. Bringing staff in early saved us a second round of card sorting, and building shared components early kept five people's screens looking like one product.
</Step>
