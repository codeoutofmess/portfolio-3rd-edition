<Step n="01" title="Why I built it">
I went through this myself. Nobody puts the admin after you land in order, in plain language, for your visa. There are good tools for what your visa allows. This covers what comes after it's granted.
</Step>

<Step n="02" title="Careful scope">
It isn't immigration advice, which is a legal line in NZ. Every item links to a government page, and it keeps as little personal data as it can, because the people using it are new here.
</Step>

<Media src="/images/work/first90-nz/checklist-view.webp" caption="Each item shows the cost, the time it takes and where the rule comes from." />

<Step n="03" title="Tone is the design">
Government admin reads like compliance. Here each item gives you the short answer, the cost and the time up front, with the why one tap away.
</Step>
