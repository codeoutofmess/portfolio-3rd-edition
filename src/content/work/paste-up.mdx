<Step n="01" title="The gap">
Eventfinda and UnderTheRadar already cover ticketed shows well. Free and DIY nights, like Eyegum's Wednesdays or Pyramid Club, end up scattered across Instagram stories instead.
</Step>

<Step n="02" title="Submissions, not scraping">
Scraping Instagram breaks easily and risks the platforms' terms. So venues and artists submit their own listings with a shared access code, and each one waits for my approval before it goes up.
</Step>

<Media src="/images/work/paste-up/submit-flow.webp" caption="Submitting a gig takes a shared code, not an account." />

<Step n="03" title="A flyer wall">
Anton and Space Mono, hard borders with offset shadows, and a flat ink, paper and pink palette. It should feel like the DIY tier, not a cheap ticketing clone.
</Step>

<Media src="/images/work/paste-up/architecture.svg" caption="Pages talk to Supabase directly. A small API was added later for ConsoleView." />

<Step n="04" title="Where it stands">
Listings, submissions, moderation and venue pages are all built. No venues are on it yet; reaching out to them is the next step. Three of its screens were also rebuilt in Angular as [ConsoleView](/case/consoleview).
</Step>
