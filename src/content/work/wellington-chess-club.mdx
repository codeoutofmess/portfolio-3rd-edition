<Step n="01" title="What I noticed">
I'd been to a few Thursday nights at the club. The site was fast and friendly, but 15 years of results, from 2011 to 2026, sat in hand typed pages with no way to search them.
</Step>

<Step n="02" title="Keep what works">
No tracking, a named committee you can reach and a busy Instagram. None of that needed changing. The gap was the archive.
</Step>

<Media src="/images/work/wellington-chess-club/results.jpg" caption="The latest results, pulled from the new archive." />

<Step n="03" title="Fifteen years of drift">
Results had been typed in at least four formats. My script works out which one a page uses, parses it and checks it. Anything it can't read with confidence stays as it was, because a misread result is someone's game reported wrong. 95 tournaments and 154 crosstables came across.
</Step>
